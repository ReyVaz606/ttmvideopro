Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

TTM Assistant (the iPad app and the free Mac companion app) respects your privacy. In short: we do not collect any data about you.

What we collect

Nothing. TTM Assistant does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising
  • No data sent to us or to any third party

How the app works

The iPad app talks only to your own Mac, over your local network, to send your button commands to your editing software. This connection stays between your own devices. Your buttons and settings are saved on your own iPad and Mac.

Local network

iPadOS will ask for permission to find devices on your local network. This is only used to find and connect to your Mac running the TTM Assistant companion app.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
